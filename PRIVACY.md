# Privacy policy: 2048 Dark Edition

Last updated: 25 November 2026

2048 Dark Edition can be played completely offline. It has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

## What stays on your device

Scores, statistics, achievements, settings and saved games are stored on your device. If Android backup is on, Google may include them in your device backup. You can erase your stats and saved games any time in **Settings > Reset stats on this device**.

## What is sent if you join the world ranking

The world ranking is optional. Nothing is sent until you choose a player name and a flag. If you join, our server stores:

- the player name and country you chose (public)
- a random player ID, and a hash of a random key kept on your device
- your best verified scores: mode, score, top tile, number of moves, duration and time of submission (score and top tile are public)

To check that a score is genuine, the app sends the list of moves from that game. It is replayed on arrival and not stored.

If you report another player's name, the two player IDs and the time of the report are stored.

Your IP address is used briefly for rate limiting and is handled by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, to deliver and protect the service. We don't store it with your profile. The country is the one you pick; your location is never read.

We don't sell or share this data. All traffic is encrypted in transit.

## Deleting your data

Open **Settings > Delete online profile**. Your name, country, scores and reports are removed from the server straight away. If you no longer have the app, open an issue at https://github.com/krish2248/2048-dark-apk/issues with your player name, flag and best score, or use the contact details on https://sonikrish.com.

## Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13. Please don't use your real full name as your player name.
